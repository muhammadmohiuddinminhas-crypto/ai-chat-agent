Data Deletion Instructions

AI Chat Agent

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Your Right to Request Data Deletion

Users of AI Chat Agent may request deletion of personal information associated with their accounts, including account details, conversation history, and integration information.

2. How to Submit a Request

To request deletion of your information:

  1. Send an email to aichatagent.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your AI Chat Agent account.
  4. Briefly confirm that you want your account data deleted.

3. Processing Your Request

We may contact you to verify your identity before processing a deletion request. After verification, we will review and process the request within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements.

4. Information That May Be Deleted

  • Account information associated with your account.
  • Conversation messages and AI responses.
  • Message usage information.
  • WhatsApp and e-commerce integration settings stored by the application.

5. Information That May Need to Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, or needed for legitimate security purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable requirements.

6. Contact

For account or data deletion questions, contact:

Email: aichatagent.support@gmail.com

You can also review our Privacy Policy.
